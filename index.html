I am currently a Research Fellow at Singapore University of Technology and Design, working with Georgios Piliouras and Antonios Varvitsiotis. I received my PhD in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science from Institut Polytechnique de Paris (Télécom Paris), advised by Pascal Bianchi and Walid Hachem.

Research

Policy Optimization and Multi-Agent Learning

My research lies at the intersection of optimization, reinforcement learning, and game theory. I develop foundations and algorithms for policy optimization in strategic and dynamic environments. My current interests include policy optimization for reliable LLM post-training and learning and incentive design in agentic markets.

Research

Publications

Policy Optimization and Reliable Post-Training

I study policy optimization when objectives extend beyond standard expected additive rewards. My work designs and analyzes policy gradient methods for general utility reinforcement learning and related nonlinear objectives, with global optimality guarantees. More recently, I have studied objective design and policy optimization for LLM post-training, where related nonlinear objectives arise in practice. Going forward, I am interested in foundations and algorithms for reliable reasoning, including sequential calibration and the use of calibrated uncertainty to guide inference.

Multi-Agent Learning in Strategic and Dynamic Environments

I study the learning dynamics of multiple adaptive agents interacting in strategic and evolving environments under limited feedback. My work develops foundations and algorithms for learning in static, Markov, and dynamical games, with guarantees for convergence, equilibrium learning, and regret in settings that include adversarial disturbances and limited or preference feedback. Going forward, I am interested in coupled learning and incentive design for interacting AI agents acting on behalf of different principals in agentic markets.

Broader Foundations in Optimization and Learning

I also study online learning and stochastic optimization. In particular, my recent work studies online learning with hidden-convex losses, including under bandit feedback. This perspective connects to my work on general utility reinforcement learning and Markov games, where apparently nonconvex policy-space objectives inherit favorable structure from their underlying occupancy-measure representations.

My earlier PhD work studied adaptive gradient methods for nonconvex stochastic optimization through dynamical systems and stochastic approximation perspectives.
